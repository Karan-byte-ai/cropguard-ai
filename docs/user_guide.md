# CropGuard AI - User & Field Operator Handbook

## 1. Introduction
CropGuard AI is designed for farmers, field scouts, and agronomists to quickly detect plant diseases from leaf photographs, receive certified guidance, and monitor regional outbreaks.

---

## 2. Scanning a Plant Leaf

### Photo Capture Tips:
1. **Focus & Framing**: Place a single affected leaf inside the circular framing guide.
2. **Lighting**: Take photos in bright, indirect daytime light. Avoid harsh shadows or strong backlight.
3. **Distance**: Hold the camera 15-25 cm from the lesion spots.
4. **Cleanliness**: Wipe dust off the phone camera lens.

### Live Feedback Indicators:
- **Quality Good / Hold Steady**: Green indicator. Tap the capture button.
- **Too Dark**: Amber warning. Move toward open daylight or rotate angles.
- **Too Blurry**: Red warning. Tap the screen to refocus and hold steady.

## 3. Understanding Your Diagnosis

### Results Components:
- **Disease Name & Crop**: Top-ranked pathogen identified by the neural vision network.
- **Confidence Rating**:
  - `High (>85%)`: Strong visual match. Full advisory displayed.
  - `Medium (55-85%)`: Multiple candidate diseases shown. Recommended to submit a second photo.
  - `Low (<55%)`: Unknown / ambiguous symptoms. General care guidance and KVK extension contact provided.
- **Grad-CAM Heatmap**: Slide the viewer handle left and right to inspect the exact areas that triggered the AI decision.
- **Advisory Sections**:
  - **Symptoms**: Confirmation hallmarks.
  - **Organic Management**: Certified bio-agents (Trichoderma, neem oil, copper).
  - **Chemical Control**: Exact labeled dosages with pre-harvest intervals (PHI).
  - **Prevention**: Spacing, irrigation, and rotation rules.
- **Audio Readout**: Tap **"Listen to Advisory"** for spoken guidance in Hindi, Marathi, or English.

---

## 4. Multilingual & Offline Mode

- **Switching Languages**: Use the globe dropdown in the header to switch between English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Marathi (मराठी).
- **Offline Queuing**: When mobile data drops in rural fields, scans are automatically saved into local device storage. They sync automatically as soon as connectivity resumes.
